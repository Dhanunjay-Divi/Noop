NOOP gives you a private view of the health and fitness signals your supported wearable already captures.

See sleep, recovery, activity, heart-rate, HRV, respiratory, blood-oxygen, temperature, workout, and long-term trend information when compatible inputs are available. NOOP keeps measured, imported, and estimated values clearly distinguished and leaves missing inputs missing.

Your core history is stored locally on your device. There is no required NOOP account or NOOP-operated cloud. Optional features—including Apple Health, supported wearable links, manual import of compatible Oura, Fitbit, or Garmin export files, an OpenAI-compatible coach, and your own self-hosted server—remain under your control and request permission before use.

NOOP supports local export and backup so you can keep a portable copy of your history. Widgets and Live Activities can show selected information at a glance when you enable them.

NOOP is developed independently and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any wearable manufacturer.

NOOP is intended for general wellness and fitness information. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose or treat any condition, and is not a substitute for professional medical advice or emergency services. Feature availability and background delivery depend on your hardware, permissions, data quality, and operating-system scheduling.
