# NOOP v9.2.0

This private-preview update focuses on evidence, calm automation, and a Today-first reading flow. It
does not claim proprietary WHOOP score parity and does not turn experimental wellness estimates into
medical diagnoses or treatment advice.

## Today and metric detail

- Every metric opens on its current/latest reading, followed by the user's personal comparison,
  selectable history, related signals, and education.
- Today, Stress detail, and Trends reports use the same source-isolated, causal autonomic-load model.
  A carried older value keeps its real as-of date rather than being relabeled as today.
- Readiness wording is observational. The discredited acute-to-chronic ratio is removed; bounded
  daily Effort can provide a simple variety note but never enters additive training-load math or
  silently downgrades the physiology state.
- Profile shows BMI to one decimal with an explicit screening limitation. Optional target weight is
  chosen entirely by the user; NOOP does not recommend a target or rate of change.
- Health Monitor summarizes only finite, calibrated readings. Missing values and raw optical SpO2
  ADC never count as outside a range.

## Conservative automation

- Activity suggestions on the lock screen are opt-in and reconciled against current candidates,
  settings, age, and handled state. Weaker detections stay in-app for review.
- Stress nudges need verified stillness, enough clean R-R windows, a stable warm-up baseline, and no
  active workout or breathing session. Current sources without trustworthy motion do not auto-nudge.
- Post-workout summaries are a separate opt-in local notification. They run after wearable sync,
  seed existing history silently when enabled, and keep workout health details inside NOOP.
- Widget and Live Activity freshness comes from the sensor packet clock, not from an unrelated app
  refresh. Stale and duplicate surfaces are ended.

## Performance and build reliability

- Android no longer blocks first composition on the active-device Room query or opt-in WorkManager
  schedule repair. Hidden Workouts and Insights destinations stop collecting while inactive, deep
  workout projections are memoized, and liquid canvases are capped at 60/30/20 fps.
- On the same Pixel 2/API 35 emulator across eight cold launches, median launch time moved from
  approximately 1.465 seconds to 1.064 seconds, an approximately 27% improvement. This emulator
  measurement is not physical-device performance evidence.
- Fresh Android installs now match the Apple OLED Black default while preserving every existing
  appearance choice. iPhone SE Key Metrics headers keep their full labels without changing card size.
- Unsigned, profile-less Apple builds fail closed before calling HealthKit APIs that require an
  entitlement. App Store/TestFlight receipt and embedded-profile capability paths remain distinct.

## Data and upgrade safety

- Existing databases migrate in place. Sleep motion-quality provenance is preserved so confidence
  cannot be inflated by sparse samples.
- Re-import cleans only exact legacy derived-stress rows from the retired importer behavior. It never
  broad-deletes metrics or another source's data.
- Portable backup settings schema v4 adds only the optional user-selected target weight; older
  readers continue to ignore additive fields safely.
- Personal Team builds still expire after seven days; reinstall the freshly signed app over the same
  bundle ID to preserve the local container.
