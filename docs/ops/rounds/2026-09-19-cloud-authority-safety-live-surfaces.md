approved a staged cloud-authoritative target. Preserve reliable offline
collection and immediate Safety behavior while moving canonical durable
history, formula publication, recommendations, and cross-device state toward
the managed service. In the same round:

## Scope

### In scope

- A durable owner decision and staged migration contract for cloud authority.
- Server-side canonical metric publication foundations with versioned
  provenance, shadow parity, rollback, and no destructive authority flip.
- Managed Safety repeat-until-response policy, private push payloads, urgent
  platform presentation controls, acknowledgement stop conditions, and
  synthetic/PostgreSQL tests.
- Signed-in macOS viewer foundations without creating a second BLE collector.
- Per-device live-heart-rate presentation controls and battery-aware update
  bounds on Apple and Android.
- Managed Friends directional consent and summary projection verification.
- Narrow metric-first, loading, provenance, first-run, More, Account, Data &
  Sync, and accessibility improvements justified by current source.
- Existing WHOOP compatibility retained for comparison testing.
- Supplier-neutral adapter/runbook work; no vendor binary import.

### Non-goals

- Imitating a government Amber Alert, bypassing mute or Do Not Disturb without
  the required platform entitlement and explicit user consent, or claiming
  emergency-service dispatch.
- Enabling automatic medical/fall inference.
- Uploading real health, contact, or location data to staging.
- Removing all phone state. The encrypted short-lived collector journal,
  offline working cache, immediate Safety initiation, and pending upload state
  remain necessary edge responsibilities.
- Claiming real APNs/FCM receipt, background wake, audible presentation,
  Dynamic Island appearance, Mac push, BLE continuity, haptics, band battery,
  step accuracy, metric accuracy, or phone/band battery life without matching
  signed physical-device evidence.
- Shipping or redistributing the owner-supplied vendor SDK before rights,
  security, exact-model, SBOM, privacy, and physical gates pass.
- Windows implementation in this round.
